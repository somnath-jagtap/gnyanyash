Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

Gnyanyash is a library management software product. This policy describes, at a high level, how information may be handled when libraries and authorized users use the software.

2. Information

Depending on features used, the software may process member details, book records, issue and return records, visitor records, donation records and notification information.

3. Use

Information is used to provide library-management functionality, maintain records, operate notifications, improve reliability and support authorized users.

4. Library responsibility

Each library using Gnyanyash is responsible for ensuring that the information it enters and communications it sends are lawful and appropriate.

5. Communication

Where SMS or WhatsApp notifications are enabled, recipient contact information may be processed to deliver those communications through the configured provider.

6. Security

Gnyanyash is designed with access controls and tenant separation so that operational data is associated with the relevant library. No online system can guarantee absolute security.
